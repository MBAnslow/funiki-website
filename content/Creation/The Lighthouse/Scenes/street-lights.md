This is the first scene in The Lighthouse demo. It provided an opportunity for players to get to know eachother while playing their roles as their characters.

There are a few different stages to this scene:

1. Travelling along a road at night. Periodically passing by street lights.
2. Standing infront of the bright front lights of the taxi before it departs.
3. The taxi departing. Its rear lights making the players glow red which fades at it drives away.

The taxi scene uses very simple lighting elements. A single faint directional overhead light and 2 lights periodically passing by. You can see what is captured from the virtual scene on the right and which lighting information is sent to the lights on the left.

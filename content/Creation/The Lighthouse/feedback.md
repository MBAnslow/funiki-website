We will dive into some of the specific feedback across two sessions to provide a broad qualitative analysis based on group questions that occured at the end of both sessions. We asked them to reflect on what they liked or didn't like but also what they imagined was happening. We recorded the audio on a mobile phone and used noScribe to create transcriptions.

There are a couple of important biases related to our participants to note here. Most participants hadn't tried table-top RPG sessions before, so it's hard to disentangle the Funiki-specific enjoyment from the general table-top RPG experience. Furthermore, most participants had a tech background which might bias them to be interested in the technology in general and overlook the bleeding edge of technology.
